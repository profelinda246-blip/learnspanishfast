Un perro que habla español

Listen and read along. In a few minutes you’ll understand a whole story in Spanish, even if you’ve never studied a word.

  1. 1
    Just listenPress play and listen once. Don’t read yet, and don’t worry about every word.
  2. 2
    Listen and read alongPlay it again and follow the story below. The words in red are from your list of 20.
  3. 3
    Check yourselfRead it once on your own, then go back to your PDF and try the 5 questions.
ListenUn perro que habla español · 1:48

Hay un hombre. El hombre se llama Tom. Tom vive en California. Tom tiene un perro. El perro se llama Taco. Taco es un perro pequeño. Taco es un perro muy, muy pequeño.

Tom tiene un problema. Tom quiere hablar español, pero Tom no habla español. Tom está triste. Tom está muy triste.

Taco mira a Tom. Taco dice: “¡Guau! ¡Guau!”

Tom dice: “Taco, ¿tú hablas español?”

Taco dice: “¡Claro que sí! Yo hablo español.”

Tom no dice nada. ¡Su perro habla español! Tom dice: “Taco, yo quiero hablar español.”

Taco dice: “No hay problema. Vamos a México.”

Tom y Taco van a México. Van a un restaurante. En el restaurante hay una mujer. La mujer se llama Rosa.

Rosa dice: “Hola. ¿Qué quieren?”

Tom quiere hablar español, pero Tom habla un poco de español. Solo un poco. Tom mira a Taco. Taco mira a Tom.

Tom dice: “Yo quiero… yo quiero… ¡un taco!”

Rosa mira al perro. El perro se llama Taco. Rosa no está feliz.

Taco dice: “¡No, no, no! ¡Yo no!”

Rosa dice: “¿El perro habla español?”

Tom dice: “Sí. Mi perro habla español. Y yo hablo un poco de español.”

Rosa dice: “¡Qué bueno!” Rosa le da a Tom un taco. Tom come el taco. Tom está feliz. Muy feliz.

Taco no está feliz. Taco también quiere un taco.

Fin.

Read it in English (only after you’ve tried!)

There is a man. The man’s name is Tom. Tom lives in California. Tom has a dog. The dog’s name is Taco. Taco is a small dog. Taco is a very, very small dog.

Tom has a problem. Tom wants to speak Spanish, but Tom doesn’t speak Spanish. Tom is sad. Tom is very sad.

Taco looks at Tom. Taco says: “Woof! Woof!”

Tom says: “Taco, do you speak Spanish?”

Taco says: “Of course! I speak Spanish.”

Tom doesn’t say anything. His dog speaks Spanish! Tom says: “Taco, I want to speak Spanish.”

Taco says: “No problem. Let’s go to Mexico.”

Tom and Taco go to Mexico. They go to a restaurant. In the restaurant there is a woman. The woman’s name is Rosa.

Rosa says: “Hi. What would you like?”

Tom wants to speak Spanish, but Tom speaks a little Spanish. Only a little. Tom looks at Taco. Taco looks at Tom.

Tom says: “I want… I want… a taco!”

Rosa looks at the dog. The dog’s name is Taco. Rosa is not happy.

Taco says: “No, no, no! Not me!”

Rosa says: “The dog speaks Spanish?”

Tom says: “Yes. My dog speaks Spanish. And I speak a little Spanish.”

Rosa says: “How great!” Rosa gives Tom a taco. Tom eats the taco. Tom is happy. Very happy.

Taco is not happy. Taco wants a taco too.

The End.

You did it. ¡Felicidades!
