The five levels

What each class expects of you on day one

01

Beginning I

Nothing at all. True beginners welcome, as are refugees from high-school Spanish who never got speaking.

02

Beginning II

You can speak on familiar topics in the present tense with acceptable pronunciation, roughly what Beginning I gives you.

03

Intermediate Fall

You understand most of what you hear in class and can hold a simple conversation without translating first.

04

Intermediate Spring

You finished Intermediate Fall, or you can already narrate a short story in the past tense.

05

Intermediate-Advanced

You comprehend spoken Spanish easily and want conversation and reading at pace, not more grammar.
