All classes · 2026-27 schedule

Five levels. One weekly class. No textbook.

Every class is 100% comprehensible Spanish, built on stories and a short novel. Choose the level at which you can speak, not the level at which you can understand. Classes fill fast, so register early.

Level 01 · Start here

Beginning I

For true beginners, and for anyone who's taken Spanish before but still can't speak it. You acquire the top 100+ verbs and words through TPRS storytelling and finish able to talk about yourself in the present tense.

You are ready for this level if:

You want everyday spoken Spanish and are happy to start from the beginning. Beginning I & II are recommended back to back.

Level 04 · Part 1 of 2

Advanced I

An advanced, guided conversation course held entirely in Spanish: personal and content-related topics across the past, present, future and subjunctive. No outside written work — the whole class is spoken. This is the first half of a two-part course that continues directly into Advanced II.

You are ready for this level if:

You understand the subjunctive and can already communicate in most tenses, and have Profe Linda's approval. Advanced I & II are taken back to back, as one continuous course.

Before the first class

Required materials for every student

1

Order your class novel

Each level has its own short Spanish novel — see the class card above for the exact title. Order the paperback as soon as you register; it's where the highest-frequency phrases and top 100+ vocabulary come from.

2

Order the LSF curriculum book

Each level also has its own curriculum workbook on Amazon — again, see the class card above for the link once it's posted.

3

Get your classroom supplies

A set of multi-colored highlighters, a black dry-erase marker, and a 9x11 dry-erase board. Cheap and reusable across every level.

4

The $5 audiobook subscription

A one-year audiobook subscription costs $5 and can be purchased after your first class. It covers almost every class novel and makes the read, read, read part of the homework easy. Register it at TPRSBooks and you get their whole library, in any language — choose your own password and write it down, Profe Linda cannot recover lost passwords.

Please buy the paperback rather than printing an ebook. Printing ebooks violates copyright, and the fine runs to $250,000. Gracias.
